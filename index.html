The first week: Shopify's review period

The offers launched while Shopify was testing post-purchase offers more strictly, after recent checkout-hijacking incidents involving third-party apps. For about a week Shopify showed post-purchase offers only some of the time, so fewer buyers saw them. Even so, the Checkout upsell kept selling through that week.

Shopify lifted the restriction after about a week, and results picked up:

Thank-you page upsell

Shopify never shows a post-purchase page to buyers who pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Amazon Pay or Sezzle. To reach them, we added an offer to the Thank-you page on 2 Nov. Every buyer now sees an offer, whatever way they pay.

That reach is what the holiday offers build on.
